"@utrecht/open-forms-container-css": minor
---

Style more of Open Forms in `utrecht-open-forms-container`, so sites no longer need their own CSS for it: content components with the `info`, `warning`, `error` or `success` custom class as a spotlight section, radio button labels, the summary data list spacing, and no padding around the form card and progress indicator. For formio.js forms, also style disabled textboxes as read-only and the read-only content of a textarea as multiline data. Add a mixin for each part, such as `utrecht-open-forms-container-content`.
