Form controls have a 2px border on all sides. `utrecht.form-control.border-width` is now `utrecht.border-width.md` (2px) instead of `utrecht.border-width.sm` (1px), and the 3px `utrecht.textbox.border-bottom-width`, `utrecht.textarea.border-bottom-width` and `utrecht.textarea.border-block-end-width` values are removed, so the bottom border has the same width as the other sides.
